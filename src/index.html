  1. Open settings and enter the final part of your LinkedIn profile URL.
  2. Compose and format your post in the editor.
  3. Save useful drafts as snippets for later reuse.
  4. Copy the converted post, open LinkedIn, and paste it into the composer.
  5. Review the final post on LinkedIn before publishing.

Your posts, snippets, and settings are stored only in this browser. There is no cloud sync.

The hosted site may collect anonymous, aggregate usage and performance metrics; composer content is not included.

LinkedIn's composer does not natively support rich text. This free, open-source tool converts a focused set of formatting into characters that survive pasting.
